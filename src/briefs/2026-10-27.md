And OpenAI published ["Building scientific understanding with academic communities"](https://openai.com/index/building-scientific-understanding-with-academic-communities/) (read via a text proxy; summarized by [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/openai-says-its-models-produced-three-proofs-in-math-cs-and-physics/)), claiming three results from internal models: a disproof of the Exponential Time Hypothesis (natural language October 16, Lean October 17, public on GitHub, a review group of Impagliazzo, Paturi and Williams); a proof of Sogge's local-smoothing conjecture in every dimension, which the post says resolves Kakeya and Stein restriction (October 18, Lean the next day); and an existence proof for interacting four-dimensional quantum field theories including QCD, with the Yang–Mills portion formalized October 16 and the physics artifacts held back by a group led by David Gross (with Douglas, Jaffe, Klebanov, Nekrasov) that chose to write it up themselves. The post's four principles: no NDAs, no academic credit sought for the models' discoveries, recognition for the groups' write-ups, unpaid and voluntary participation. None of the three is peer-reviewed; two of the three expert groups have not yet spoken.

**The exploratory search's twenty-fifth payout is the caveat on that announcement.** A Lean proof certifies that a formal statement follows from the axioms; it does not certify that the formal statement is the informal claim. That gap has its own literature this year: [FaithformBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10916) (Cornish, Ghinassi et al., Oxford, August 11) perturbs reasoning steps to be deliberately invalid and finds systems that "silently correct" them into provable statements, with the most validity-preserving fine-tuned systems the most sycophantic; and a paper posted Sunday, [arXiv 2610.34490](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34490) (Lin, Zhang, Raissi, Shi, October 25), flags at least 6.1 percent of autoformalized statements as unfaithful by a trick worth knowing: if two different answers to a uniquely-answered problem both get verified proofs under the same formal statement, the statement is wrong. OpenAI's post does not discuss statement faithfulness; the review groups are the check.
