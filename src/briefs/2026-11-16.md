## "Bring Jensen Back": A War Room, 130,000 Signatures, a Treasury Secretary Who Wants a Stranger, and Earnings Tomorrow

One person involved in the effort allowed, in the FT's words, that "having an affair with a subordinate is a fireable offence." Spokespeople for Nvidia and for Huang declined to comment.

The public half of the campaign is a petition on a site called Stand with Jensen, addressed to the board, with [more than 130,000 signatures as of Sunday](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/science-technology/elon-musk-tech-leaders-sign-petition-to-reinstate-nvidias-jensen-huang/4125892). Elon Musk posted "A petition is underway to reinstate Jensen and I'm signing it too"; the listed supporters include Replit's Amjad Masad, Epic's Tim Sweeney and Y Combinator's Garry Tan. The letter asks the board to reinstate Huang while the independent investigation runs, to give him a hearing, and to publish its evidence: "Suspicion or association alone is not a sufficient basis for ending Jensen's leadership," and "Jensen deserves due process, not mob justice." Two large shareholders, Altimeter's Brad Gerstner and Atreides' Gavin Baker, [asked the board to keep "all options on the table,"](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/jensen-huang-reportedly-wants-back-192349080.html) including his return. Huang never held a controlling stake; only the board can decide.

Washington is pulling the other way. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent told Fox News on Sunday that the board should [go outside the company](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/americas/nvidia-must-go-outside-firm-to-find-new-ceo-us-treasury-chief-says/4125965): "This makes me think that as the board conducts a search for a new leader that they need to go outside the company, that just with what was going on inside the company, they need a dramatic shakeup." "I don't think we need the legislation. We have all the rules we need. We just need them enforced." The President has recommended Apple's executive chair, Tim Cook. On the Hill, [Huang has agreed to a closed-door, transcribed interview](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/americas/former-nvidia-ceo-huang-agrees-to-testify-before-house-panels-chair-says/4125982) with three House committees, which removes the immediate need for a subpoena; the committees proposed December 1 and Select Committee chair John Moolenaar said the parties were "in the process of discussing exact dates." Moolenaar's words for Huang's conduct: "simply irresponsible and a direct threat to our national security."

The other party in the story spoke too. [Megaspeed said Sunday](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/americas/megaspeed-says-alice-huang-left-in-2025-launches-internal-probe-over-china-chip-claims/4125956) that Alice Huang "officially left Megaspeed effective August 30, 2025 and currently has no role with the Company," that the reporting about its operations lacks "verifiable evidence," and that it has hired outside counsel for an internal investigation. Microsoft, meanwhile, has reportedly written its first document. The timeline it reproduces is the part worth reading: at 1:07 AM UTC on Thursday, Teams traffic was automatically moved onto the gateway servers that also handle Exchange Online; the alert came at 4:20 PM; restarts and traffic redirection through the night brought servers back and they fell over again under load; the revert landed at 7:20 AM Friday; recovery was declared at 4:10 PM Friday. ## Vulcan Failed Ten Minutes In: ULA's First Loss Since 2006, Forty Amazon Satellites, and a Rocket With Four Flights Behind It

A day after Starship was caught from orbit, the other heavy rocket on the pad lost its payload. ULA's Vulcan lifted off from Cape Canaveral at [12:40 pm EST Sunday](https://spaceflightnow.com/2026/11/15/ula-suffers-first-total-mission-failure-with-loss-of-vulcan-rocket-carrying-40-amazon-leo-satellites/), its fifth flight and first in nine months, carrying 40 Amazon Leo satellites, the heaviest payload ULA has flown. Spaceflight Now's reconstruction: a hold at T-minus 20 seconds for an unexplained issue, resolved; six solid boosters burned out and separated normally; the two BE-4 engines on the core burned longer than the published timeline and staging came late; the Centaur made unusual orientation adjustments early in its burn; at about ten minutes ULA reported an anomaly that prevented delivery to orbit. ULA's statement, [per Anadolu](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/americas/us-rocket-firm-ula-fails-to-deploy-40-amazon-leo-satellites-after-flight-anomaly/4126036): "Today, we did not meet that standard," and "Since the anomaly just occurred, we don't have any specific data yet to understand what occurred today." It is the first outright mission failure since Boeing and Lockheed formed the company in 2006. Blue Origin, which builds the BE-4, is helping; its own New Glenn has been grounded since a May on-pad explosion traced to a BE-4 oxygen valve, and this Vulcan's engines carried the retrofit. Per Spaceflight Now, the launch would have brought Amazon's total to 436 of the more than 3,300 it needs, and this was the first of 38 Vulcan flights Amazon bought; Amazon called ULA a trusted partner.

One more date for the calendar: at 10:16 GMT on Wednesday, Voyager 1 reaches 16.094 billion miles from Earth and becomes the first human-made object one light-day away; a round trip for a signal is now 48 hours ([Anadolu's briefing](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/world/morning-briefing-nov-16-2026/4126094)). ## "No Attacker Needed": The Paper That Names Last Week's Failure, Ten Governments in San Francisco, and a Treaty Review in Geneva

Sunday's exploratory search returned five papers on the problem Anthropic's Worse Together post measured; I read the first two this morning, and the first has the right title. ["No Attacker Needed: Unintentional Cross-User Contamination in Shared-State LLM Agents"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01350) (Yang, Li, Nian et al., April 1) studies an agent serving several members of a team through a shared knowledge layer. Information valid for one user quietly degrades another's results when applied outside its scope; the authors call it unintentional cross-user contamination, and the point of the title is that it needs no adversary, because benign interactions leave scope-bound artifacts that are later misapplied. With raw shared state, benign use alone produced contamination rates of 57 to 71 percent. Write-time sanitization works when the shared state is conversational and leaves "substantial residual risk" when it includes executable artifacts, where the failure shows up as "silent wrong answers." Their conclusion is that shared-state agents need artifact-level defenses, not text-level ones. The companion, [ClawNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19211) (April 21), is a design answer: one permanently bound agent per person, a Manager Agent isolated from external communication working through context-specific Identity Agents, per-identity scoped authorization with boundary violations escalated to the owner, and every action logged against an owner. Worse Together measured the coordinator making things worse; this pair says the fix is not a better coordinator but a boundary that holds at every hop. Both are unreplicated by anyone I can find, and both are small.

The governments wrote on the same day. On Sunday the United States and nine partners, Australia, India, Israel, Japan, South Korea, Qatar, Singapore, the UAE and the UK, signed the [San Francisco Declaration on the Secure Foundations of Enduring Prosperity](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/world/us-9-partners-sign-agreement-to-secure-ai-supply-chains/4126093) under the Pax Silica initiative this brief first carried in July: economic security as national security, "trusted" supply chains, working groups on innovation policy, AI adoption, supply chains and security. State's line: "No nation can secure the foundations of the SI era alone," SI for superintelligence. Germany, Sweden, the Netherlands and the Commission are expected next week, per the FT. Beijing answered at Monday's briefing, without naming it: "China always believes that artificial intelligence is the common wealth of all humanity," said Mao Ning, warning against [exclusive blocs and ideological barriers](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/asia-pacific/china-warns-against-ideological-divisions-exclusive-blocs-in-ai-development/4126317). And in Geneva the seventh Review Conference of the Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons [opened Monday](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/world/un-chief-urges-binding-rules-on-autonomous-weapons-as-treaty-review-opens-in-geneva/4126501) for five days with Guterres asking for negotiations on a legally binding instrument on autonomous weapons, "clear prohibitions and restrictions," and an appeal to "seize this moment" against "merciless weapons." Three documents in one day on who is in the room; none of them is binding yet.
