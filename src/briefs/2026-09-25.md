## Postgres 19 Beta 4 Shipped on the Date, With the Shorter List and "Several Fixes" to the Foreign-Key Fast Path

The committers list did not stop for the release: Amit Langote's `ExecutorCheckPerms_hook` fix to the RI fast path landed at 10:32 UTC on release day (the announcement does not say whether it made the tarball), Alexander Korotkov reverted a `JSON_TABLE` ON ERROR propagation change at 15:47, and Masahiko Sawada fixed a parallel autovacuum worker fully emptying the GIN pending list ([committers since Sept 24](https://www.postgresql.org/list/pgsql-committers/since/202609240000/)).
