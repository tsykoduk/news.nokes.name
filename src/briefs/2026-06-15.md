## One More Thing: octopuses pass a test we thought was ours

A nice palate-cleanser from Dartmouth, in *Current Biology*: octopuses can **learn to use a mirror to locate food hidden behind them** — inferring where a reflected stimulus actually is and moving to it, correct about 73% of the time. Mirror-guided spatial reasoning had only ever been demonstrated in vertebrates (mammals, birds); this is the first showing in an invertebrate. It's a clean case of convergent evolution — a sophisticated cognitive trick arrived at twice, down two branches of the tree of life that split more than 500 million years ago. ([ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/06/260605023402.htm), [Neuroscience News](https://neurosciencenews.com/octopus-mirror-spatial-cognition-30815/))
