**The antecedent, exploratory.** Sunday's Public Benefit Fund is a voluntary transfer of 5% of the company's equity to a vehicle the company owns. The compelled version already exists on paper: Jeremy Bearer-Friend (George Washington) and Sarah Polcz (UC Davis), ["Sharing the Algorithm: The Tax Solution to Generative AI,"](https://law.stanford.edu/stanford-legal/the-case-for-a-public-share-in-ai/) in the Columbia Journal of Tax Law, discussed on Stanford Legal in June: "systemically important AI firms should pay a tax with stock," into a public fund, as an addition to existing taxes rather than a replacement, with the burden falling on existing shareholders through dilution and a CalPERS-style fiduciary structure to keep the stake away from executive control. The two designs answer the same question with opposite governance: in one the public holds the shares through a trustee it can sue; in the other the company holds them through a subsidiary and will publish the rules later.
