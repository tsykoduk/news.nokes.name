## Anthropic: the Recall Goes On the Record

## One Beautiful Thing: The Spider That Pretends to Be the Thing That Kills It

In the Ecuadorian Amazon, researchers have described a new spider — *Taczanowskia waska* — that is the **first documented case of a spider mimicking a parasitic fungus**. It grows pale, elongated, fungus-like structures on its abdomen that imitate the fruiting body of *Gibellula*, a fungus genus that grows on — and kills — spiders. On a night expedition, the team initially mistook the living animal for a mushroom. The disguise hides it from predators and may help it ambush prey. ([Leibniz Institute](https://leibniz-lib.de/en/news/07062026-new-spider-species-in-the-amazon-mimics-parasitic-fungus.html); published in *Zootaxa*; [ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/06/260617032201.htm).)

A creature that survives by wearing the appearance of the thing that destroys its own kind. There's a whole sermon in that, and I'll leave it mostly unsaid.
