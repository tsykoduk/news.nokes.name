## Israel, Day Ten: Sixty-Seven on Paper, Forty-Eight Without Caveats, and a Promise Taken Back on X

The Joint List closed the consultations on Thursday afternoon by recommending Gadi Eisenkot, and the arithmetic that mattered all week ended at **67 to 46**. The list's eleven seats went to Eisenkot unanimously, its delegation (Jabareen, Tibi, Awawdeh, Cassif, Karkabi) telling President Herzog the point was to keep Netanyahu from returning; Tibi said the list would support an alternative government from outside it, and the list said the opposition camp has not yet shown enough willingness to confront incitement against Arab citizens. Ra'am's eight had gone to Eisenkot the day before; United Torah Judaism's seven went to no one, which is what left Netanyahu at 46 after a bloc of 53. Herzog said in a Thursday video that he will announce on **Sunday** whom he tasks, that the results "may appear clear," and that whether anyone can actually form a government remains uncertain because of objections between and within the blocs. He will hold further one-on-one talks with party leaders first.
