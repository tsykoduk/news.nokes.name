## The Recall Grows a Rulebook — and a Deadline

Fable 5 and Mythos 5 are still dark on day 14. **A deadline, today.** On June 18 a bipartisan group of four members of Congress sent Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick a letter demanding a written explanation of the legal basis for the suspension — by **June 26**. The letter pins the mechanism down as an "is informed" letter under the Export Control Reform Act's emerging-and-foundational-technology authority (the EAR §744.22 route). For two weeks this has been an action with no published rules; today is the first forced moment of *show your work*. Whether Lutnick answers, and how specifically, is the thing to watch this morning. ## One Quiet Wonder: The Queen Is Built, Not Fed

For generations the story was simple — feed a larva enough royal jelly and you get a queen. A study in *Nature* (early June) says the diet was never the whole answer. Young worker bees construct specialized peanut-shaped "royal cribs" out of softer, warmer, moisture-retaining wax, staffed by a previously unrecognized caste of "queen cell builders," and *that engineered environment* is what decides whether a larva becomes royalty. ([UC Riverside](https://news.ucr.edu/articles/2026/06/03/how-honeybees-really-crown-their-queens), [phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2026-06-honeybees-crown-queens.html), [Earth.com](https://www.earth.com/news/honeybees-palace-like-nurseries-for-future-queens/))
