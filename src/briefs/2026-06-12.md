**The read:** the settlement that's "agreed" is the *divisible* half — Hormuz reopens, oil flows, relief gets dialed against compliance. That's the same "relief for performance" structure that's been on the table since May, and it's the part that can be split into a schedule. The *indivisible* remainder — sovereignty over the nuclear program — is exactly where the standing IAEA breach and Iran's centrifuge defiance are pulling the other way. A 15–20-year no-enrich pledge is an attempt to make the indivisible thing divisible by stretching it across time; whether that holds is the whole game. Worth remembering: Trump's announcement is undatelined and unsigned, Iran is publicly downplaying it, and a tanker crew died today. The honest posture is describe-the-state and flag-the-volatility — not bet on the terminus.
