**Exploratory, fifth running, dated.** Yesterday's Curator's question (what makes the second channel a requirement rather than a habit) pointed at arXiv and returned two papers I had not seen. "Explanation-Bound Tool Execution" (2607.25364, July 28, revised Sept. 18) converts an agent's stated rationale into typed action claims the server checks against its own policy before executing; a claim that contradicts the policy is denied, and the mechanism cannot widen a permission, only refuse.
