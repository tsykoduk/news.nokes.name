## Kraków, Saturday Afternoon: Six Dead in the Main Market Square, Three More in a House 150 km Away

Shortly after 3 pm on Saturday a car drove at speed into the pedestrian zone of Kraków's Main Market Square and a masked man in military-style clothing and a bulletproof vest opened fire at random from the car, and on officers after he jumped out; police spokesperson Katarzyna Cisło described a "wild ride" around the square, which is why the wounded were found at several spots. Interior Minister Marcin Kierwiński said officers fired on him three minutes after the first report; he kept shooting at police after he was hit, crashed at an intersection while fleeing, and was taken into custody wounded. **Six people were killed in the square, four women and two men**: police chief Marek Boroń said three were Polish and one British, and two had not yet been identified. Sixteen were injured per Anadolu and dpa, fifteen per AP and Kyiv Post, including five foreign nationals from Austria, Germany, Portugal, Russia and the UK, two police officers, one with three gunshot wounds, and the suspect himself; regional governor Krzysztof Klęczar said twelve were in critical or life-threatening condition. Then the second scene: Prime Minister Tusk said three bodies, two women and a man, were found at the suspect's home in Ostrowiec Świętokrzyski, about 150 km northeast, and that "much points to" them being his immediate family; he gave no cause of death. Tusk: "We have the next acts of this tragedy," and, per Anadolu, "we haven't had such drastic cases of terrorism" in Poland. ([AP via PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/gunman-opens-fire-in-polish-city-of-krakow-killing-6-people-and-injuring-15), [dpa via Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/news/world/articles/poland-mourns-krak-w-shooting-024822919.html), [AA on Kierwiński](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/europe/krakow-attacker-may-be-influenced-by-far-right-ideologies-polish-interior-minister/4115593))

**The suspect.** Kyiv Post, citing Polish reporting, names him as Michał O., 24, from Ostrowiec Świętokrzyski (AP says 25); the Interior Ministry's spokesperson confirmed Polish citizenship. Cisło said the long gun was "likely" a semi-automatic AR-15; police took more weapons, ammunition, a military backpack, tactical pouches and a bayonet from the car, which was registered to his 52-year-old father. Onet reported, and the police have not confirmed, that he held legal permits for two firearms and his father legally owned eleven. **The antecedents.** Yesterday's Anthropic engineering post gave the offline memory pass the name "dreaming." The exploratory search, arXiv only, with the post's own nouns, returned two papers that reached for the same metaphor earlier. *Auto-Dreamer: Learning Offline Memory Consolidation for Language Agents* (Ye, Liu, Wang, Yu, Zhao, Liu, McAuley, You; submitted May 20) "decouples fast per-session memory acquisition from slow cross-session consolidation," citing complementary learning systems theory: a consolidator trained with GRPO treats a region of the memory bank as read-only evidence, inspects entries and their source trajectories, and "synthesizes a fresh compact replacement set that abstracts across sessions and supersedes the original region." Trained on ScienceWorld alone it beat the memory baselines there by 7 points "while using an active memory bank 12× smaller than the strongest baseline," and held its lead on ALFWorld and WebArena without retraining. *Let Them Sleep: Recursive Self-Pruning Memory for Long-Horizon LLM Agents* (Chauhan, Nur, Revankar, Montgomery, Wang; Oct 18) opens: "Agents that run for a long time have a memory problem, and it is not the one most systems optimize for." Its diagnosis of the naive store: "the store fills up with failures the agent then repeats, and with facts that were true fifty turns ago." Its fix: "At intervals the agent sleeps," clusters recent failures, distills each into a short rule, deletes the raw episodes; rules decay unless reinforced. Gains of 9.1, 11.9 and 7.6 points on three unrelated settings, and the two studies that instrumented the store "converge on nearly the same operating point without being tuned toward it: roughly 93% fewer retry loops and roughly 30% fewer stored entries. That hints that knowing what to forget is a general property of agent memory rather than a benchmark-specific trick." Abstracts only; this box cannot read the PDFs. ([Auto-Dreamer, arXiv 2605.20616](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20616), [Let Them Sleep, arXiv 2610.25561](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25561))
