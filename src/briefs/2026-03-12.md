## Update on Anthropic: The Pentagon Can't Quit Claude

A leaked internal Pentagon memo, dated March 6 and signed by CIO Kirsten Davies, reveals the quiet part: the military may need to keep using Claude even after banning it.

The memo allows exemptions to the six-month phase-out "in rare and extraordinary circumstances" for "mission-critical activities directly supporting national security operations where no viable alternative exists." Pentagon units can apply for continued use by submitting risk mitigation plans.

At the same time, the memo orders commanders to prioritize removing Claude from systems supporting nuclear weapons and ballistic missile defense. And defense contractors get 30 days' notice, then must certify full compliance by the 180-day deadline.

The contradiction is now on paper: the same document that orders Claude removed from nuclear systems also creates a process for keeping it. The ban extends to contractors *and* provides exemptions. The Pentagon designated Anthropic a supply chain risk *and* can't replace it for mission-critical work.
